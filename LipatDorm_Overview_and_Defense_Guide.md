# LipatDorm: Project Overview & Defense Guide

## 1. Project Title
**LipatDorm: A Peer-to-Peer Micro-Logistics and Circular Marketplace Platform for Student Relocations**

---

## 2. What is it all about?
LipatDorm is an all-in-one web and mobile platform designed specifically for college students and budget renters. It solves the chaotic, expensive, and wasteful process of moving out of dormitories and boarding houses. It combines a **budget-friendly micro-hauling service** (for moving bags and boxes) with a **"Pass-It-On" circular marketplace** (for selling or giving away bulky furniture you can't bring home). 

It is completely peer-to-peer, meaning it connects students with local drivers and other students directly—**no landlords required.**

---

## 3. Who are the users?
The system has three primary users:
1. **The Mover (Outgoing Student):** Students moving out of their dorm who need cheap transport and need to get rid of heavy items.
2. **The Seeker (Incoming Student):** Freshmen or current students looking to buy or claim cheap/free pre-loved dorm furniture.
3. **The Micro-Hauler (Driver):** Local owners of small utility vehicles (Multicabs, L300s, sidecars) looking for side-income without joining massive corporate logistics companies.

---

## 4. What is its goal?
**Primary Goal:** To provide a safe, standardized, and affordable relocation system for students while completely eliminating move-out waste.

**Academic & Global Goal:** It directly aligns with the United Nations **SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production)**. 
* *Target 12.5:* It diverts usable, bulky furniture from municipal landfills by keeping items in circulation through the student community.
* *Target 12.2:* It minimizes fuel waste and carbon emissions by right-sizing vehicles and allowing students to share cargo space.

---

## 5. The Two Core Features

### Feature A: Micro-Hauling Logistics
A booking system for short-distance, small-scale moves that bridges the gap between unreliable tricycles and overly expensive 4-wheel commercial trucks.

### Feature B: The "Pass-It-On" Circular Market (Trade)
A hyper-local marketplace tied directly to a student's move-out countdown, allowing them to sell, trade, or give away items they cannot transport home.

---

## 6. The Process of Both Features

### Process A: How Micro-Hauling Works
1. **Inventory Input:** The student selects the items they want to bring (e.g., 4 boxes, 1 chair).
2. **Vehicle Matching:** The system calculates the volume and recommends the exact vehicle needed (e.g., Multicab), avoiding the cost of a truck that is too big.
3. **Upfront Pricing:** The system calculates a standard, transparent fare based on distance (No more arbitrary "kontrata" bargaining with drivers).
4. **Move & Track:** The student is paired with a verified local driver, tracks them on the map, and completes the move safely.

### Process B: How the Pass-It-On Market Works
1. **List & Countdown:** 2 weeks before moving out, the student lists items they don't want to bring (e.g., Desk Fan for ₱400). The item displays a red "Move-Out Countdown" tag.
2. **Make an Offer:** A buyer sees the item and clicks "Make an Offer" (e.g., offering ₱250).
3. **Acceptance & Pickup:** The seller accepts the offer. The buyer walks to the dorm and picks up the item before moving day.
4. **Eco-Tracking:** The system records the transaction and adds the item's weight to the platform's "Landfill Diversion / SDG 12 Tracker."

---

## 7. Answering the "What-Ifs" (Defense Edge Cases)

**What if the buyer just waits for the item to become free instead of buying it?**
* **Answer:** We implemented the **"Make an Offer"** and **"Watchers"** features. If a buyer waits, another student who actually needs it will buy it or make an offer first. Scarcity and high demand in student areas ensure good items sell long before they become free. Furthermore, price drops are controlled by the seller, not automatic.

**What if nobody buys the item, and moving day arrives?**
* **Answer:** In the final 24 hours, the seller can click **"Switch to Free Bayanihan Claim"**. The item becomes ₱0, and an urgent notification is sent to students in the same building/radius. College students quickly claim free items.

**What if the item is broken, or literally NO ONE claims it even for free?**
* **Answer:** The system provides an Ultimate Safety Net: **The Scrap/E-Waste Dispatch**. With one click, the system notifies a verified local junk shop or scrap collector (*magbobote*). They will pick up the item for recycling. This ensures zero items are illegally abandoned on the sidewalk, completing the SDG 12 mission.

**What if the landlord doesn't want to hold the items for the students?**
* **Answer:** The system does not require landlord collaboration. It is strictly a student-to-student handover. All pickups must happen *before* or *on* the seller's move-out date. The app acts as a marketplace, not a physical storage locker.
