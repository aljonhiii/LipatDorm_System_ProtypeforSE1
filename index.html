MOVE SMART.
SPEND LESS.
WASTE LESS.

LipatDorm is a budget-friendly micro-moving and cargo-sharing platform designed for dormitory students and renters.

02 — The Problem

MOVING SHOULD NOT BE THIS EXPENSIVE.

01

Expensive

Large moving trucks are too expensive for short-distance dormitory moves. Students often overpay for capacity they don't need.

02

Inefficient

Students often need multiple trips using small vehicles, wasting time, money, and fuel on repeat journeys.

03

Wasteful

Students abandon or throw away usable furniture and appliances because transporting them feels too difficult or expensive.

03 — The Solution

ONE PLATFORM.
A SMARTER WAY TO MOVE.

LipatDorm combines right-sized vehicles, cargo-sharing, and a community reuse marketplace — all in one platform.

01
Right-Sized Rides
Match your cargo with appropriately sized local vehicles — multicabs, pickups, or vans. Pay only for what you need.
02
Cargo-Sharing
Students moving in similar directions share one vehicle and split the cost. Save more, move smarter.
03
Pass-It-On
Give usable furniture and appliances a new owner instead of throwing them away. Reduce waste, support community.
